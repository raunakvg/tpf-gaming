TROUBLESHOOTING FIELD LAG

Identifying whether the problem is hardware, routing, or signal noise.

Troubleshooting Matrix

The 3 Types of Lag

1. Computer Lag

Symptom: Screen stutters or freezes.
Cause: Weak GPU or overheating computer. (Not ISP fault)

2. Speed Delay (Ping)

Symptom: Click button, action happens 0.5s later.
Cause: Long physical routing road trip to game server.

3. Lost Data (Packet Loss)

Symptom: Character teleports around screen.
Cause: Information lost in transit over Wi-Fi interference.
